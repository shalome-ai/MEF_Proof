*Opened 8 Nov 2026. Each entry: the result, where it was deferred from and why, what the paper would contain, its premise set, and status. Entries are candidate papers of the series beginning with M1 and M2; they are not ledger rows and carry no rigour tag until a canonical site exists.*

**Contents of the paper.** (i) The Dirac spectrum at a cone point of angle π on the pillowcase with the Spin^c structure L = 𝒪(3) and corner lift (−1)^{mn} (M1 Prop 7.3, Cor 7.4); the smallest case (λ_{0,2} = π) by hand and a second route. (ii) The spectral rigidity Σ²(L) / Δ₃(L) of the full pillowcase spectrum and its placement relative to the Poisson and GUE benchmarks. (iii) The bridge: a theorem identifying the field-level ω with the spectral functional, or an explicit statement of the hypothesis under which they coincide. (iv) The spectral face of the A4 orbit-average construction that M2 uses for the uniform-phase zero (M2 R5.1), if the bridge needs it.

**Premise set.** M1 (geometry) and M2 (field content and equation) as citable premises; EXT spectral theory of cone manifolds and random-matrix rigidity. No other MEF paper.

**Dependencies.** M2 published or at least frozen (ω's definition and the parity theorem); M1 Cor 7.4.

**Status.** Registered; not scheduled. Owner: PI.
