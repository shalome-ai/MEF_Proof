# Rulings in force

*The PI's rulings on the theory that are in force for the Mx series, each with its Decision Log entry. Opened 11 November 2026 when CLAUDE.md was rewritten from zero (Decision Log P49, P50). This file states what is in force; the Decision Log holds the PI's words and the reasons. A ruling is added here when it is made and struck here, with the entry that struck it, when it is withdrawn.*

**Status: sections 1–3 agreed by the PI for M1 (P52, P53); section 4 holds drafts (P52). Proposed by the architect, 11 November 2026. Compiled from CLAUDE.md §§0a and 5 as they stood and from Decision Log P41–P49, without reading anything of the withdrawn M2 v1.0. The PI banks by commit.**
