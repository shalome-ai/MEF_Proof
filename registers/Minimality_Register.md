# Minimality Register — the Mx series

*M2 v1.0 was withdrawn on 10 November 2026 (Decision Log P41) and is in `superseded/M2_v1/`. Entries below that cite M2, its work packages (`M2_WP…`), its certificates or `papers/M2/…` notes are records of the withdrawn paper: they are not in force and are not premises of the new M2.*

*Cumulative register of every point at which the series takes the minimal option among those the axioms leave open (the standing principle, PI ruling 8 Nov 2026; M1 Definition 3.2 extended to every datum of the theory). Opened 8 Nov 2026 with M2. One entry per choice. Each entry records the choice space, the option taken, the criterion, where it is stated, and what would count as the minimal option failing — so that if anything downstream breaks, the suspects are listed. Entries are in good standing until a failure is logged; a failure is also logged in the Corpus Errors register. PI banks; architect proposes.*

**Columns.** # · Paper/node · Choice space (what was open) · Minimal option taken · Criterion (M1 Def 3.2 tier or its extension) · Stated at · Failure test (what would falsify this choice) · Status.
