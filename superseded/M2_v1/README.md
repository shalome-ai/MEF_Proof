# M2 v1.0 — withdrawn 10 November 2026 (Decision Log P41)

M2 v1.0 ("The Master Equation from four axioms", canon 9 November 2026) was written on M1 v2 with four axioms. By the PI's ruling it is scrapped and M2 is written from scratch on M1 v5.0 with five axioms, as if v1.0 had never existed. Everything of v1.0 is kept here as a record and is **not a premise, not a starting text and not to be read by the session that writes the new M2 until its own derivation is written and recorded**; only then is the new result compared with the old (CLAUDE.md §2b.2).

- `canon/` — the paper, its work-package files and PDF, as they stood in `canon/M2/`.
- `lean/` — the seven certificates, as they stood in `MEFProof/Paper_M2/`; removed from the library build.
- `sow/` — the build plan of v1.0.
- `audit/` — the step audit of v1.0, the notes on ζ and the reading notes of 10 November 2026, with their check scripts. Paths such as `papers/M2/STEP_AUDIT.md` in earlier notes and registers refer to files now here.
