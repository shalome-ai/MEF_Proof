### S01b · m1 120 · Definition~7.4 (flux and states) · M1 v7.3 ll.832–840

- **Head:** structures: the twist, the states, the corner signs
- **As it stands in M1:**
```tex
A \emph{twist of the fibre} of \emph{flux} $d\geq1$ is the line $\mathcal{O}(D)$ on $E$ of a divisor $D=\sum a_{i}P_{i}$ on the corners, with $a_{i}\geq0$ and $\sum a_{i}=d$. A twist of flux zero is a flat line with holonomy $\pm1$: the trivial one, or one of the three \emph{half-turn twists}.

A \emph{state at rest} is a holomorphic section of the twist: for $d\geq1$, a function $f$ on the covering torus $E$ whose poles are bounded by $D$. The fold acts on states at rest by $f(z)\mapsto f(-z)$ or by $f(z)\mapsto-f(-z)$, its two lifts, called here the first and the second. A \emph{state on the pillowcase}, for a given lift, is a state at rest that the lift leaves unchanged. The torus $E$ is the fibre before it is folded, and the pillowcase is the fibre folded (Theorem~\ref{thm:fibre}); for a given lift, the states of the folded fibre at rest are the states on the pillowcase for that lift, and a state at rest on $E$ that the lift does not leave unchanged is not one of them. Which lift is called the first depends on the divisor chosen for a line, and the statements below speak of one lift or the other.

The \emph{corner sign} of the twist at $P_{i}$ is $(-1)^{a_{i}}$, the action of the first lift on the fibre of $\mathcal{O}(D)$ there. The four signs are taken up to one overall sign. A corner is \emph{odd} if its sign differs from that of the other three.

The \emph{structure} of a twist is its flux together with its corner signs up to the overall sign or, for flux zero, its holonomy. It is this that clause~(ii) of Definition~\ref{def:A5} speaks of. The drive is not a holomorphic map of $E$; it acts on structures through its action on the corners.

Two states on the pillowcase, for the same lift of the fold, have the same shadow: they differ only in the internal piece. The states at rest of Definition~\ref{def:A5}(iii) are states on the pillowcase for one of the two lifts of the fold. This is the reading of Definition~\ref{def:A5}(iii).
```
