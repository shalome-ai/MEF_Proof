## Where M2 starts: the five steps the PI agreed (P69), word for word
1. M1 fixes the kind of shape, not its proportions. The pieces, the twists and every whole number are fixed. The relative sizes of the pieces, the warp and the aspect ratio of the fibre are not.
2. The sixth axiom says those cannot be free. Clause (a): nothing is left to be chosen (Def 3.2). So something inside the shape must set them.
3. A rule that sets them is a field equation. M1 already leans on it: it speaks of "the stationarity conditions of the geometry's energy functional" (Def 3.1, l.258) and never says what that functional is.
4. So the first job of M2 is to say what that functional is, built from the shape alone. This follows the PI's words: for the equation, the sixth axiom says that nothing is put in besides the one object.
5. "Small movements around the shapes" is then the method. Stationary means that small movements make no difference to first order. This is the standard calculus of variations (RECALLED, to be read at source before use).

The PI: "Doing step 1 would mean that you read the canon version of M1".

**Standing of the five steps.** They are the PI's direction on where to start. The words are the previous architect's, agreed by the PI. They are a plan and not results: each is checked against canon M1 before it becomes a premise (CLAUDE.md §4). Flags to carry into the assumption list and the step audit:
- Step 1 is to be made exact from canon M1: a list, with lines, of every continuous datum M1 leaves unfixed.
- Step 3 identifies the functional that M1 names at l.258 with the equation of M2. M1 does not say so. It is an identification step (CLAUDE.md §5).
- Step 5 is RECALLED. No external theorem is used until it is read at source and entered in Verified References.
- "Small movements around the shapes" allows two meanings: moving the points along the shape, and changing the shape itself. Which is meant, or whether both, is asked of the PI, not inferred. The PI's earlier words on this, a lead: "you are creating a vector field".
- The five steps were proposed by a session that knew something of how the withdrawn M2 was derived. The start is therefore not shown to be independent of the withdrawn paper. Enter this in the step audit.

## Found in canon M1 v6.3 by the previous session (READ; each to be re-read, not taken on trust)
- M1 says the warp's law, which piece is scaled and by a function on which, is "fixed where the warp is given its law, with the field equation" (l.127). M2 owes this.
- M1 speaks of "the geometry's energy functional" and its stationarity conditions (Def 3.1, l.258; the deflationary reading, l.992) and does not define the functional.
- The declared input of M1's theorem is that the aspect ratio of the fibre is fixed; it "would fail if the stationarity conditions of the geometry's energy left it free" (l.992).
- M1 says of its sequel that "the two sheets of the involution of A2 and the non-commutative structure of A3 are the variables in which that equation is written", and names "the Coherent Field" (l.1028). This is a sentence of M1's outlook about M2. It is not a result.
- ψ and ω appear in M1 as labels only: the periods (L_ψ, L_ω) with τ₂ = L_ω/L_ψ (l.602) and the coordinates (θ_ψ, θ_ω) (l.1075). They do not appear as fields.
- M1 does not define when two geometries are the same.
- Declared in M1 and not derived: the count of drives in clause (a), in two parts; that every symmetry of the torus is given an element on spinors (Cor 7.8).
