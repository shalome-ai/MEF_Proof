- A shape has four parts: g (how lengths are measured), V (the flow), Q (the quaternionic structure, spinors and Clifford action), ∇ (connections and Hermitian metrics on the two lines). M1 states no relation between g and the others. (N1)
- The fibre's shape cannot be the same at all times: the gluing by M₀³ keeps no flat shape. "The aspect ratio" of M1 l.602 is a path in time, not one number. (N1 §4; CHECKED under two suppositions)
- The law of natural growth at one rate cannot hold across a closed piece. So the second axiom's growth runs along time. (Step audit; CHECKED)
- With the growth in the block of time and space, which M1 says is modelled on the quaternions (ll.615, 943), all three clauses of M1's Definition 2.3 are met, and the swap is the inverse of a quaternion. This is a reading of a point M1 leaves to M2 (l.127). No proof, finding or part of the shape of M1 changes. (Step audit, R-b and its corrections)
- Forms (a) and (b) are different shapes. (b) is the quaternions with their own flat lengths. (b) and "the internal piece shrinks" are the same up to a unit chosen afresh at each moment. (Step audit)
- Of the candidates in M1 for the field, only the state has the closing-up, the fold, the quaternions, the drive and the shadow all acting on it. (P79; N3 §2)
- **Requirement Q4:** at rest on a fibre, the stationary fields of M2's equation must be exactly M1's states: three, two kept by the fold. M1 fixed this before M2 exists. (N3 §4)
