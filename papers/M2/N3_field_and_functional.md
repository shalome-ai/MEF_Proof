## 2. What the field is
**Identification (DECLARED; the architect's; step audit 0.8; not ruled).** The field is the *state* of M1, no longer required to be at rest and no longer confined to one fibre: a section, over the whole shape, of the bundle whose sections over a fibre at rest are M1's states.

Ground, all READ:
- M1's second numbered choice: "an object … is a state of the internal piece at rest, and its shadow what is left of it when the internal piece is forgotten" (l.163).
- The quaternions act on it: "at the spinor level, as the algebra generated by the Clifford action on a spinor bundle over M" (l.214).
- The fold acts on it, in two ways (l.719).
- The drive acts on it, as the element −w (l.837).
- It returns to itself round the time circle (l.847).

What M1 fixes of that bundle (READ): a Spin^c structure exists and its line is O(3) on CP² (l.693); on the fibre the twist has flux three with one odd corner (l.763); the states at rest are the holomorphic sections of the twist, three of them, of which the fold keeps two (ll.719, 731, 763).

What M1 does not fix:
- **The integer on S²** (owed, P77). The bundle is not fully named without it. To be discussed with the PI (P79).
- That states are spinors. M1 says "for the canonical Spin^c structure the states at rest are the harmonic spinors of the fibre. We use this as motivation … and the counts below do not depend on it" (l.728). M2 would rest on it, so M2 owes it: read at source and entered in Verified References (node N2).
- The quaternions act at the tangent level too (l.214). A field at that level is the alternative to this identification. M1 defines an object as a state (l.163), which is why the spinor level is taken.

## 3. What it means for the field to satisfy each axiom (proposed; each a numbered choice of M2)
M1 defines what it is for a *geometry* to satisfy each axiom. For the field M2 needs its own six. Each below follows the M1 lines given; where M1 leaves a real alternative it is named.

| Axiom | Proposed meaning for the field | From M1 | Alternative left open |
|---|---|---|---|
| A5 shadow | The field is defined over the whole shape. At each point of time and space, its dependence on the internal piece is its unseen part. Two fields that differ only in the internal piece have the same shadow | ll.150–158, 725 | **What the shadow of a field is, as an object on time and space, is not defined.** M1 defines the shadow of a point and of a state at rest only. Not needed until something is computed from a shadow |
| A1 closure | There is one field on the whole closed shape: carried once round any loop it returns to itself. Round time: under the gluing M₀³ with the element (−w)³ = 1, and with the shape larger by the factor of one circuit | ll.178, 837–863; P81 | Whether the field itself is multiplied by a factor after one circuit, as the lengths are |
| A2 growth and decay | The field creates the growth: what grows as time advances, and at what rate, is fixed by the field through the equation. The swap carries a field with its growth to a field with the matching decay | l.190; P79, P84 | — (ruled) |
| A3 order | The quaternions act on the field and the order of two actions matters: away from the corners no commutative part of the algebra is enough to describe it. At the four corners it reduces to ℝ, ℂ_i, ℂ_j, ℂ_k | ll.206, 214, 521 | — |
| A4 drive | The drive carries the field: one step acts by M₀ on the fibre and by −w on the field. The field is not carried to itself by one step | ll.232, 837; cf. l.156 | "Never stands still" could instead be asked of the flow only, as M1 does, and not of the field |
| A6 oneness | There is one field: one section of one bundle, not several kinds. Nothing else enters the equation | P76 | — (ruled) |

## 4. What any functional must satisfy (fixed before a functional is chosen)
| # | Requirement | Ground | What would show a candidate fails it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Its value is the same for shapes that are the same: unchanged by an admissible relabelling | M1 l.258; N1 | A relabelling that changes the value |
| Q2 | It gives overall size no meaning: its stationary points are unchanged when every length is multiplied by one number | M1 ll.255, 258, 271 | A stationary point that fixes an overall size |
| Q3 | It is built from the shape and the one field and nothing else: no second field, and no constant that is not a whole number of M1 or one of π, e, φ as M1 derives them | P69 step 4; P76; M1 ll.110–118 | Any other ingredient |
| Q4 | **At rest on a fibre, its stationary fields are exactly M1's states:** the holomorphic sections of the twist of flux three, three of them, two kept by the fold | M1 ll.719, 731, 763 | A candidate whose fields at rest are fewer, more, or other than these |
| Q5 | The swap of growth and decay carries stationary points to stationary points | M1 l.187 ("two faces of one thing"); l.190(iii) | A stationary point whose swapped image is not one |
| Q6 | Stationary points are compatible with the drive: with the gluing M₀³ round time and the element −w | M1 ll.837–863 | A stationary point that does not close up round time |
| Q7 | No modulus survives: modulo sameness, the stationary points are isolated | M1 ll.258, 264 (clause (a)) | A family of stationary points that are not all the same |
| Q8 | The growth, what grows and its rate, is fixed at a stationary point by the field | P79, P84 | A stationary point at which the growth is free, or is fixed without the field |
| Q9 | M1's shape, with every piece, twist and whole number, is a stationary point | P69 steps 3–4; M1 l.992 | No stationary point on M1's shape |

**Q4 is the sharpest.** M1 has already counted the states at rest. Any equation M2 writes must give those back and no others. It is a test fixed by M1 before M2 exists.

**Clause (a) "can exclude and cannot pick" (M1 l.271).** Q1–Q9 exclude. If more than one functional passes all nine, something further must select, and what that is has not been argued. Open.

## 5. What is not done here
- The functional is not chosen and no candidate is named.
- No external theorem is used. The ones a candidate would need (the operator the quaternions' action defines on the field; that its fields at rest on the fibre are the holomorphic sections) are for node N2.
- The six meanings of §3 are proposals. None is entered in a register.

## 6. RECALLED (used nowhere above)
- On a surface with a complex structure, the harmonic spinors of the canonical Spin^c structure twisted by a line are the holomorphic sections of that line together with a second space that vanishes when the degree is positive.
- The first Chern number of the tangent bundle of S² is two.
