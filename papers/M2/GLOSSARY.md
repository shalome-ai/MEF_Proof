# M2 — glossary (M2's own entries)

**What this is (CLAUDE.md §6; `tools/PAPER_FORMAT.md`, "Glossary"; Decision Log P173, P177).** The terms and names that M2 introduces, in the order of first use. Opened 18 November 2026 (Decision Log P183), with no entry.

**Rules.**
- "The glossary" of M2 is two things together: Appendix B, "Names and glossary", of `canon/M1/Paper_M1_Six_Axioms_v7_3.tex`, and then this file. M1's entries are not copied here and none of them is changed. They are read where they stand.
- Before a name or a term is used in M2, both are looked at, and the term is used as it stands there. One thing has one name, in M2 and across the series.
- A term that M2 introduces is defined by the architect at its first use, as a sentence of the paper: an entry of `papers/M2/WRITE_LOG.md`, logged and confirmed as CLAUDE.md §10 says. Its glossary entry is added here in the same chat.
- An entry is: the term; the words that define it, copied as they stand from the entry of the write-up log that states them; and where they stand, which here is that entry's number. When the paper is assembled the entry is made by script from the body of the paper, by exact match, and its place is the printed place.
- An entry is not revised afterwards. The PI (P177): "You define when it is first used, it goes into Glossary, that becomes canonical, any future reference refers to the Glossary. Glossary is carried forward to Mx+1 , new terms are added. There is no backwards revision".
- The same entry is copied, in the same chat, into M2's section of `registers/Definitions_Register.md`, with the number of its write-up log entry. This file is the canonical text and the register row is its copy.
- When the paper is assembled, its appendix "Names and glossary" is M1's entries, copied by script from M1's source, followed by the entries of this file.

**Format of an entry.**

    - **term** · "the defining words, copied" · M2-Wnnn

## Entries

- **state (of the geometry)** · "A \emph{state} of the geometry is a section of the spinor bundle of M1, Definition~2.7, over $\Mtwelve$." · M2-W005
- **lift of the fold** (to a line on the covering torus; to the spinor bundle of the fibre) · "A \emph{lift of the fold} to $N$ is a continuous map $\lambda\colon N\to N$ that carries the fibre of $N$ over each point $p$ of $E$ to the fibre over $\iota(p)$, is linear on each fibre, and applied twice is the identity." … "the \emph{lift of the fold} to it that $\lambda$ defines is $\iota^{*}\otimes\lambda$, where $\iota^{*}$ is the pull-back of functions and $(0,1)$-forms by the fold, as in M1, Section~7.4 ($\iota^{*}\mathrm{d}\bar z=-\mathrm{d}\bar z$)" · M2-W022
- **sign** (of a lift of the fold, at a corner) · "At a corner $P_{i}$, which the fold leaves in place, $\lambda$ acts on the fibre over $P_{i}$ as multiplication by a number whose square is $1$; this number, $+1$ or $-1$, is the \emph{sign} of $\lambda$ at $P_{i}$." · M2-W022
- **covering torus carried round time** $Y$ · "The \emph{covering torus carried round time} is the space $Y$ of the pairs $(p,s)$, $p\in E$, $s\in\R$, in which $(p,s+3L)$ and $(M_{0}^{3}p,s)$ are one point." · M2-W039
- **corner circles** (of $Y$) · "The fold acts on $Y$ by $(p,s)\mapsto(\iota(p),s)$, and it leaves in place exactly the points of the four \emph{corner circles}, $\{(P_{i},s)\colon s\in\R\}$ for $i=1,\dots,4$." · M2-W039
- **lift of the fold** (to a line on the covering torus carried round time) · "A \emph{lift of the fold} to a complex line bundle $N_{Y}$ on $Y$ is a continuous map $\lambda_{Y}\colon N_{Y}\to N_{Y}$ that carries the fibre of $N_{Y}$ over each point $y$ of $Y$ to the fibre over the image of $y$ under the fold, is linear on each fibre, and applied twice is the identity." · M2-W039
- **sign** (of a lift of the fold to a line on $Y$, on a corner circle) · "On each corner circle $\lambda_{Y}$ acts on every fibre as multiplication by one and the same number, $+1$ or $-1$, the \emph{sign} of $\lambda_{Y}$ on that circle." · M2-W039
- **family of complex structures carried round time** · "A \emph{family of complex structures carried round time} is, for each $s$, a complex structure $J_{s}$ on $E$ as in Definition~\ref{def:lift}, with a coordinate $z_{s}$ linear in $(\theta_{\psi},\theta_{\omega})$ and depending continuously on $s$, such that $M_{0}^{3}$ is holomorphic from $(E,J_{s+3L})$ to $(E,J_{s})$." · M2-W040
- **spinor bundle of the fibre carried round time** (and its lift of the fold) · "For such a family, and for a line $N_{Y}$ on $Y$ with a lift of the fold $\lambda_{Y}$, the \emph{spinor bundle of the fibre carried round time} is the bundle on $Y$ whose fibre over $(p,s)$ is that of $\Lambda^{0,*}_{J_{s}}\otimes N_{Y}$, the spinor bundle of Definition~\ref{def:lift} for $J_{s}$, the $(0,1)$-forms at $(M_{0}^{3}p,s)$ and at $(p,s+3L)$ being joined by the pull-back by $M_{0}^{3}$; its \emph{lift of the fold} is $\iota^{*}\otimes\lambda_{Y}$." · M2-W040
- **unfolded geometry** $\widetilde{M}$ · "The \emph{unfolded geometry} is \[ \widetilde{M}\;=\;Y\times S^{3}\times\CPtwo\times\Stwo , \] time together with the fibre before it is folded, space (M1, Proposition~7.9) and the base (M1, Theorem~7.10)." · M2-W052
- **lift of the fold** (to a line on the unfolded geometry) · "A \emph{lift of the fold} to a complex line bundle $N$ on $\widetilde{M}$ is a continuous map $\lambda\colon N\to N$ that carries the fibre of $N$ over each point of $\widetilde{M}$ to the fibre over the image of that point under the fold, is linear on each fibre, and applied twice is the identity." · M2-W052
- **sign** (of a lift of the fold to a line on $\widetilde{M}$, over a corner circle) · "Over each corner circle $\lambda$ acts on every fibre as multiplication by one and the same number, $+1$ or $-1$, the \emph{sign} of $\lambda$ over that circle." · M2-W052
- **almost complex structure of the unfolded geometry** $\widetilde{J}$ · "An \emph{almost complex structure of the unfolded geometry} is an almost complex structure $\widetilde{J}$ on $\widetilde{M}$ that is: on the directions along the fibre, a family of complex structures carried round time (Definition~\ref{def:spinortime}); on $\CPtwo$ and on $\Stwo$, their complex structures (M1, Proposition~7.3 and Section~7.4); and on the directions of time and of space, the structure $J_{0}$ with $J_{0}\partial_{s}=e_{1}$ and $J_{0}e_{2}=e_{3}$, where $\partial_{s}$ is the unit vector field along time and $e_{1},e_{2},e_{3}$ are the vector fields on $S^{3}=\mathrm{Sp}(1)$ (M1, Proposition~7.9) that left translation makes from $i,j,k$." · M2-W053
- **spinor bundle of the unfolded geometry** (and its lift of the fold) · "For a line $N$ on $\widetilde{M}$, the \emph{spinor bundle of the unfolded geometry}, for $\widetilde{J}$ and $N$, is the spinor bundle of $\sigma_{\widetilde{J}}\otimes N$; it is $\Lambda^{0,*}_{\widetilde{J}}\otimes N$, the $(0,*)$-forms of $\widetilde{J}$ with values in $N$ \cite[pp.~29 and~77]{Nicolaescu}. For a lift $\lambda$ of the fold to $N$, its \emph{lift of the fold} is $\iota^{*}\otimes\lambda$, where $\iota^{*}$ is the pull-back of the $(0,*)$-forms by the fold of $\widetilde{M}$; on the forms of time, of space and of the base, $\iota^{*}$ is the identity." · M2-W053
- **$\sigma_{\widetilde{J}}$** (the $\Spinc$ structure that $\widetilde{J}$ induces) · "Let $\sigma_{\widetilde{J}}$ be the $\Spinc$ structure that $\widetilde{J}$ induces on $\widetilde{M}$ \cite[pp.~44--45]{Nicolaescu}." · M2-W053
- **step of the unfolded geometry** $\tau$ · "The \emph{step of the unfolded geometry} is the map \[ \tau\colon\widetilde{M}\to\widetilde{M},\qquad \tau(p,s,r)=(M_{0}p,\;s-L,\;r), \] for $p\in E$, $s\in\R$ and $r\in S^{3}\times\CPtwo\times\Stwo$: on the fibre it is the step $M_{0}$ of the drive, the linear map of its matrix (M1, Section~4.1); it carries the fibre at the time $s$ to the fibre at the time $s-L$, $L$ being the length of one step; on space and on the base it is the identity." · M2-W069
- **lift of the step** (from one line on the unfolded geometry to another; from one spinor bundle of the unfolded geometry to another) · "For complex line bundles $N$ and $N'$ on $\widetilde{M}$, a \emph{lift of the step} from $N$ to $N'$ is a continuous map $\nu\colon N\to N'$ that carries the fibre of $N$ over each point $m$ of $\widetilde{M}$ linearly and one-to-one onto the fibre of $N'$ over $\tau(m)$." … "The \emph{lift of the step} that $\nu$ defines, from the spinor bundle of the unfolded geometry for $\widetilde{J}$ and $N$ to that for $\widetilde{J}$ and $N'$, is $(\tau^{-1})^{*}\otimes\nu$, where $(\tau^{-1})^{*}$ is the pull-back of the $(0,*)$-forms by the inverse of $\tau$" · M2-W069
- **unknown** (of the equation) · "The \emph{unknown} of the equation is a state of the geometry (Definition~\ref{def:state}), read through the lift of the fold: a section $\psi$ of $S_{4}$ that the lift of the fold leaves unchanged (Proposition~\ref{prop:step}(ii))." · M2-W085
- **given** (what is given to the equation) · "\emph{Given} are: the geometry of M1, Theorem~8.1, which is $\widetilde{M}$ folded (Definition~\ref{def:unfolded}), with everything that M1 fixes on it and with the continuous data that M1 does not derive held fixed as M1 holds them; the spinor bundle $S_{4}$ with its lift of the fold (Propositions~\ref{prop:whole} and~\ref{prop:step}); and the step $\tau$ with its lifts, taken as the sixth choice of this paper has them (Definition~\ref{def:step})." · M2-W085
- **equation for a state** · "An \emph{equation for a state} is a condition on the unknown $\psi$ that holds at every point of the geometry and that is made, at each point, from $\psi$, from its derivatives there and from what is given (Definition~\ref{def:unknown})." · M2-W086
- **solution** (of an equation for a state) · "A \emph{solution} of such an equation is a state for which the condition holds." · M2-W086

- **$\bar\partial$** (the operator of the twist) · "then \[ \bar\partial\sigma\;=\;\frac{\partial f}{\partial\bar z}\,\mathrm{d}\bar z\otimes e , \] a section of $\Lambda^{0,1}\otimes\mathcal{O}(D)$. On the spinor bundle $\Lambda^{0,*}\otimes\mathcal{O}(D)$ of the fibre, whose summands are $\mathcal{O}(D)$ and $\Lambda^{0,1}\otimes\mathcal{O}(D)$, $\bar\partial$ is this map on the first summand and zero on the second." · M2-W105
- **twist carried round time** $\mathcal{O}_{Y}(D)$ · "The \emph{twist carried round time}, for $D$, is the bundle $\mathcal{O}_{Y}(D)$ on $Y$ whose fibre over $(p,s)$ is that of the line $\mathcal{O}(D)$ of $(E,J_{s})$ over $p$, with the frames of Definition~\ref{def:dbar} for $J_{s}$: the constant $1$ away from the corner circles and, near the corner circle of $P_{i}$, $z_{s,i}^{-a_{i}}$ with $z_{s,i}=z_{s}-z_{s}(P_{i})$. The fibres over $(M_{0}^{3}p,s)$ and over $(p,s+3L)$ are joined by the pull-back of functions by $M_{0}^{3}$: a section that is $f$ in the frame $1$ near $(M_{0}^{3}p,s)$ is $f\circ M_{0}^{3}$ in the frame $1$ near $(p,s+3L)$." · M2-W117
- **differentiable along the fibre** (of a section of the twist carried round time; with $\bar\partial$ of such a section) · "A section $\sigma$ of $\mathcal{O}_{Y}(D)$ is \emph{differentiable along the fibre} when, in each of the frames $e$, it is $\sigma=fe$ with $f$ continuous and with derivatives along the fibre that are continuous in $(p,s)$; then \[ \bar\partial\sigma\;=\;\frac{\partial f}{\partial\bar z_{s}}\,\mathrm{d}\bar z_{s}\otimes e , \] which at each time $s$ is the $\bar\partial$ of Definition~\ref{def:dbar} for $J_{s}$." · M2-W117
- **continuous up to** (a point $q$ of a corner circle, or the corner circle; of a function and of a section of the twist carried round time; also "continuous over" the corner circle) · "A function that is defined near $q$ and away from the corner circle is \emph{continuous up to} $q$ when it has a limit at $q$, and a section is continuous up to $q$ when its function in the frame $z_{s,i}^{-a_{i}}$ is; a function or a section is continuous up to the corner circle, or over it, when it is continuous up to each of its points." · M2-W136
- **twist over the unfolded geometry** $\mathcal{O}_{\widetilde{M}}(D)$ · "The \emph{twist over the unfolded geometry}, for $D$, is the bundle $\mathcal{O}_{\widetilde{M}}(D)$ on $\widetilde{M}$ whose fibre over $(y,u,b)$ is the fibre of the twist carried round time $\mathcal{O}_{Y}(D)$ (Definition~\ref{def:twisttime}) over $y$" · M2-W152
- **restriction to the base** (of a section of $S_{D}$, over a point $(y,u)$) · "For a section $\psi$ of $S_{D}$ and a point $(y,u)$, the \emph{restriction of $\psi$ to the base over $(y,u)$} is the map $b\mapsto\psi(y,u,b)$, a form of type $(0,*)$ on $B$ with values in the vector space $V_{y,u}$" · M2-W152
- **part of type $(0,q)$ along the base** (of a section of $S_{D}$) · "the \emph{part of type $(0,q)$ along the base} of $\psi$ is the section whose restriction to the base over each $(y,u)$ is the part of type $(0,q)$ of that of $\psi$" · M2-W152
- **differentiable along the base** (of a section of $S_{D}$) · "The section $\psi$ is \emph{differentiable along the base} when each of its restrictions to the base has continuous first derivatives on $B$" · M2-W152
- **$\bar\partial_{B}$** (the operator of the base) · "Then $\bar\partial_{B}\psi$ is the section of $S_{D}$ whose restriction to the base over each $(y,u)$ is the sum, over $q=0,1,2,3$, of the part of type $(0,q+1)$ of the exterior derivative on $B$ of the part of type $(0,q)$ of the restriction of $\psi$." · M2-W152
- **restriction to the fibre** (of a section of $S_{D}$, over $(s,u,b)$) · "the \emph{restriction of $\psi$ to the fibre over $(s,u,b)$} is the map $p\mapsto\psi((p,s),u,b)$ on $E$" · M2-W153
- **differentiable along the fibre** (of a section of $S_{D}$; the term of M2-W117 is for a section of the twist carried round time) · "The section $\psi$ is \emph{differentiable along the fibre} when, in a basis of $W_{s,u,b}$, the components of each such restriction in the summand $\mathcal{O}(D)$ are differentiable sections of $\mathcal{O}(D)$ (Definition~\ref{def:dbar})." · M2-W153
- **$\bar\partial$** (along the fibre, over the unfolded geometry) · "Then $\bar\partial\psi$ is the section of $S_{D}$ whose restriction to the fibre over each $(s,u,b)$ has, as its components in that basis, the $\bar\partial$ of Definition~\ref{def:dbar}, for $J_{s}$, of the components of the restriction of $\psi$." · M2-W153
- **shadow** (of a state) · "The \emph{shadow} of $\psi$ is the shadow, as M1, Definition~2.1(i), has it, of the set of points of the geometry at which $\psi$ is not zero" · M2-W163
- **shadow after one step** (of a state) · "the set of points $(s,x)$ of time and space over which that section is not zero is the shadow of $\psi$ moved by one step in time: it is the set of the points $(s-L,x)$, $(s,x)$ in the shadow of $\psi$. It is called here the \emph{shadow of $\psi$ after one step}." · M2-W164
- **meets the fifth axiom** (of an equation for a state) · "An equation for a state (Definition~\ref{def:family}) \emph{meets the fifth axiom} if (i) it has two linearly independent solutions with the same shadow; and (ii) it has a solution whose shadow after one step is not its shadow." · M2-W165
- **smooth section of the twist** (for a divisor $D$) · "A \emph{smooth section of the twist}, for $D$, is a complex function $f$ on $\widetilde{M}$ less the points over the corner circles, smooth there, such that $w_{i}^{a_{i}}f$ extends to a smooth function across the points over the corner circle of $P_{i}$, for $i=1,\dots,4$." · M2-W175
- **smooth section of $S_{4}$** (for a divisor $D$) · "A \emph{smooth section of $S_{4}$}, for $D$, is a form $\psi$ on $\widetilde{M}$ less the points over the corner circles, with complex coefficients, smooth there and of type $(0,*)$ for $\widetilde{J}$, such that $w_{i}^{a_{i}}\psi$ extends smoothly across the points over the corner circle of $P_{i}$, for $i=1,\dots,4$." · M2-W175
- **smooth state** · "The lift of the fold acts on these by $\psi\mapsto\epsilon\,\iota^{*}\psi$, and a \emph{smooth state} is a smooth section of $S_{4}$ that it leaves unchanged." · M2-W175
- **operation of differentiation along time** · "An \emph{operation of differentiation along time} is a map $\nabla$ of the smooth sections of $S_{4}$ to themselves with $\nabla(\psi+\psi')=\nabla\psi+\nabla\psi'$ and $\nabla(f\psi)=(\partial_{s}f)\psi+f\,\nabla\psi$ for every smooth function $f$ on $\widetilde{M}$." · M2-W175
- **$\nu_{R}$** (the map of $N_{4}$ over $R$) · "There is a continuous map $\nu_{R}$ of $N_{4}$ to itself that carries the fibre over each point $m$ of $\widetilde{M}$ linearly and one-to-one onto the fibre over $R(m)$, with $\nu_{R}\lambda_{4}=\lambda_{4}\nu_{R}$." · M2-W170
