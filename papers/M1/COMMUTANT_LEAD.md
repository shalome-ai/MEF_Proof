# The lead of J1: can the quaternions that commute with Spin(12) on spinors be M1's fibre algebra?

**11 November 2026. Direction (PI):** of three ways of closing J1 the PI chose "Option 2 - test the lead first". Lead: `STEP_AUDIT.md` §11.6; Decision Log P89. This note is the protocol and then the result. **§§1–3 were written before any computation was run and before the blind reader was launched.**

## 1. The two objects
- **The candidate, ℍ_c.** All real-linear maps of the spinor space of twelve dimensions that commute with the Clifford action (the twelve gamma matrices). By §11.6 (READ, Atiyah–Bott–Shapiro p. 11) C₁₂ ≅ ℍ(32), so ℍ_c is expected to be a copy of ℍ.
- **M1's fibre algebra.** What canon M1 v6.3 itself asks of it, quoted; these are the criteria and nothing else is:

|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | "at the spinor level, as the algebra generated by the Clifford action on a spinor bundle over M" | l.214 | Is ℍ_c contained in the real algebra generated by the gamma matrices? |
| K2 | "at the tangent level, as an almost-quaternionic structure on M … anticommuting almost-complex structures I, J, K with IJ = K" | l.214 | Take ℝ¹² = ℍ³ with I, J, K. Lift them to spinors by the Clifford action. Is what they generate ℍ_c? Is it a copy of ℍ at all? |
| K3 | "Let ι act on ℍ as conjugation" (l.489); "ι acting as conjugation" (l.521) | ll.489, 521 | The fold is a half-turn of the fibre plane, an element of Spin(12). How does it act on ℍ_c? M1 needs an action that is not the identity |

**Pass** means all four. Each is reported separately. The drive is not tested: M₀ is not a rotation and has no lift through Spin(12); what M1 says of it (Ad_w, l.521) is one of its own unnumbered readings (J10).

## 2. Assumptions
1. Flat model: one tangent space ℝ¹² and its spinors. No statement is made about bundles over M₁₂. (The architect's choice of scope.)
2. "The Clifford action" is that of the real Clifford algebra with generators squaring to −1 or +1; the commutant is the same for both signs up to the type, and both are computed.
3. In K2, I, J, K are multiplication by i, j, k on ℍ³ on one side. Other almost-quaternionic structures on ℝ¹² are conjugate to it (RECALLED; not relied on beyond this model).
4. K1 is tested against M1's words as written. Whether "acts … as" may be read more loosely ("acts through") is not for this test; it is reported if the outcome turns on it.

## 3. The architect's prediction, recorded before running anything
Reasoned, not computed, so RECALLED until the script and the blind reader report:
- ℍ_c is a copy of ℍ (real dimension four).
- **K1 fails:** the algebra generated by the gammas has centre ℝ, so it meets its own commutant in ℝ only.
- **K2 fails:** the lifts of I, J, K lie in the generated algebra, do not commute with it, and generate something larger than ℍ (the spinors hold several spins of that Sp(1)).
- **K3 fails:** everything in Spin(12), the fold included, commutes with ℍ_c, so the fold acts on it as the identity.
- **K4 holds.**
- So the prediction is that the lead fails as an identification: ℍ_c is the algebra of a Spin^h structure, which M1 l.701 names and removes by clause (b).

**Failure test of the prediction:** the script or the blind reader finds ℍ_c inside the generated algebra, or finds an element of Spin(12) that moves it.

**Verdict on the lead.** K1, K2 and K3 fail; K4 holds. **ℍ_c is not M1's fibre algebra as M1 defines it.** It is not generated by the Clifford action (l.214), it is not what the tangent-level I, J, K give on spinors (l.214), and the fold cannot act on it as conjugation (ll.489, 521) because nothing in Spin(12) moves it. The prediction of §3 is confirmed in each part. What ℍ_c is: the algebra whose unit group a Spin^h structure would add, which M1 l.701 names and declines by clause (b).

**What stands from it for J1.** K4 holds for ℍ_c whether or not it is the fibre algebra: on twelve-dimensional spinors, to treat them as complex is to choose one unit of *a* copy of the quaternions, with stabiliser a circle and a two-sphere of choices. That copy is not the one M1's Sections 5 and 7 speak of.

**Found on the way, and it bears on M1 apart from the lead (K2).** Definition 2.7 has the fibre algebra act at the spinor level "as the algebra generated by the Clifford action", and at the tangent level by I, J, K. CHECKED by both: the I, J, K of the tangent level, acting on spinors through the Clifford action, do not obey the quaternion relations there and generate an algebra of dimension 30. So on the flat model the two levels of Definition 2.7 do not give the same algebra, and the spinor-level one is not ℍ (Proposition 5.2). This is the point the two readers of §11 raised from memory (their 7.7), now CHECKED. The first hypothesis is that the reading of "acts … as" is wrong: "acts through" would be consistent. It is a row for the PI beside J10, not part of J1.

**Not tested.** The drive (no lift through Spin(12)). Anything about bundles over M₁₂: the model is one tangent space. Other almost-quaternionic structures on ℝ¹² than the standard one (assumption 3).

**Failure test of the verdict.** A non-real element of ℍ_c written as a real combination of products of gammas; or an element of Spin(12) that does not commute with J.
