## C. The fifth axiom: what goes in
8. The plain sentence is the PI's: we can see the shadow, but the shadow is missing information about what it is a shadow of. The formal axiom is the existence statement (every admissible observed configuration has a smooth twelve-dimensional preimage); loss of information is a remark with proof; uniqueness is not part of it. *P19; record §2.*
9. The axiom is the centre and must do work: at least one statement or proof of M1 must fail if it is deleted. *P25; record §12; `STEP_AUDIT.md` §1.*
10. The light is Φ_QC moving round the pillowcase; that is what casts three generations in four dimensions from one object: "1 + 2 shadows". *Record §13.1.*
11. The observer is where ω > 0. The draft's clause (i), the observer at the point the drive returns to itself (P₁), is withdrawn. *Record §13.2; `STEP_AUDIT.md` D1; this supersedes P27(d), which was my decision.*
12. The warp bends the light, so that it shines in different directions and we see different shadows; the light both moves and bends. *Record §13.3 (the PI: "I think it is both").*
13. The three generations are one object and three shadows. *Record §5.*
14. ψ̄ is the chiral twin of ψ; the word "shadow" is used only in the sense of the fifth axiom. *P26(c).*
15. Chapter 8 ¶99 (the twist produces the photon and the hypercharge boson) is not assumed. *P26(e).*
16. **GAP, not an assumption I can supply:** the account gives the light, the observer and the bending in words. Without it the axiom will be idle again (item 9), and a map constructed by me to fit would be the device §0a warns against. *`STEP_AUDIT.md` §3, §5.*
