## As printed

2. `This is the tenth choice of this paper, and the seventh of this section.`
3. `; and that the spinor bundle of Definition~\ref{def:levels} is the complex spinor bundle, and data of the fibre algebra carried by spinors are complex-valued.`
4. `Clause~(b) prevails over clause~(c), so that clause~(c) does not replace a structure that needs no further object by one with a smaller integer.`
5. The hand check of the Mark of M1-W017, from "With $x=(a+a^{*})/2$ and $u=(a-a^{*})/2$" to "conversely $N(u)=-u^{2}>0$ gives $u^{2}<0$." (in full in the `.tex`).
6. `A point that neither the fold nor one step of the drive leaves in place has nothing fixed and carries $\Hquat$.`
7. `\emph{The algebra at a point.} $P_{1}$ is left in place by $\iota$ and by $M_{0}$, and its algebra is $\Fix(\text{conj})\cap\Fix(\Ad_{w})=\R$; $P_{2},P_{3},P_{4}$ are left in place by $\iota$ and not by $M_{0}$, and their algebras are $\C_{j},\C_{i},\C_{k}$ (Appendix~\ref{app:labels}); a point such as $(\tfrac13,0)$ is moved by $\iota$ to $(\tfrac23,0)$ and by $M_{0}$ to $(\tfrac23,\tfrac13)$, is left in place by neither, and carries $\Hquat$.`
8. `That the continuous data of the geometry that this paper does not derive are held fixed is the one declared input of the theorem; it would fail if a continuous datum of the geometry that this paper does not derive were not held fixed, and the geometry would then not meet clause~(a).`

9. `the torus $\Ttwo$ as the fibre of $F$ carrying the drive,` (in: "a reader who does not take clause~(c) has dimension twelve, … flux three and three steps as the least admissible values and not as the only ones")
10. `If one unit $i$ is selected, its stabiliser is $U(1)$ and the orbit of $i$ is $\mathrm{Sp}(1)/U(1)=\Stwo$.`
11. `and the tenth choice,` (in: "under the readings of Definitions~… and~\ref{def:orbits} and the tenth choice, with the continuous data …")
12. `Under clause~(c) of Definition~\ref{def:minimal} the fibre is the torus $\Ttwo$, on which $\iota$ is inversion $z\mapsto-z$ and the drive is a hyperbolic element of $\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\Z)$.`
