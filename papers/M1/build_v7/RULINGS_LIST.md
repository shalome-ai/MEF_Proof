**l.992:** The deflationary reading. The fifth axiom enters the theorem twice: as the division of the shape into the part that is seen and the part that is not, and, through its two further clauses, as the twist of the fibre. The overall size is not an input: under the sixth axiom it has no meaning. The three clauses of Definition~\ref{def:minimal} are readings of the sixth axiom and its words do not force them: a reader who does not take clause~(c) has dimension twelve, flux three and three steps as the least admissible values and not as the only ones, and a reader who allows relabellings that reverse orientation has the drive by clause~(c) alone.
