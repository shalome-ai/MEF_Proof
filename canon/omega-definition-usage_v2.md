## 2. What ω *is* (the functional)  — rigour: Motivated

ω measures **structured, mutually-held information** in a spectrum, not raw quantity of order and not raw quantity of disorder:

- **Crystalline / rigidly-ordered spectrum** → trivial correlation → ω ≈ 0.
- **Poissonian / uncorrelated spectrum** → no mutual information → ω ≈ 0.
- **GUE-like spectrum** (strong level repulsion; each mode constrains the others) → maximal structured mutual information → **ω maximal**.

This is a **one-hump complexity functional**, peaking *between* order and disorder. It is emphatically:

- **NOT entropy** (Shannon H peaks at noise), and
- **NOT negentropy** (H_max − H peaks at the crystal).

Naming ω "negentropic" was a category error: negentropy peaks where ω is *lowest*. Retire that framing wherever it appears; the corrected functional peaks in the middle.

**Computable proxy.** Spectral rigidity Δ₃(L) or number variance Σ²(L):
Poisson gives Σ²(L) = L (no rigidity); GUE gives Σ²(L) ≈ (2/π²) ln L (strong rigidity). ω tracks this rigidity — high on the GUE ridge, low toward both the crystal and Poisson limits. This is the operational handle for any attempt to *derive* ω rather than assert it.

**Precision (choose the canonical functional when formalising).** "Coherent information" (I_c = S(ρ) − S(ρ_env), a quantum-information quantity that can go negative) and "statistical complexity" (Crutchfield's C_μ, the one-hump functional) are **cousins, not twins**. When a paper needs the exact mathematical object, name it explicitly and let the other stand as gloss. Do not treat them as interchangeable inside a derivation.

## 2a. What ω *means* (the metaphysical layer) — added 8 July 2026

**This section adds an interpretation on top of §2's mathematics; it changes nothing in §2.** §2 is exact and stays the canonical formal object for any derivation. What follows is an account of *why* a one-hump structured-correlation functional is the right formal home for the thing ω is meant to name — useful for register (especially §6b, popular writing) and for keeping formalisation work pointed at the right target, but it is not itself a formalisable claim and must never be substituted for §2 inside a proof.

**The relational reading.** ω is the capacity of a system to **interact with other objects in a way that increases the coherence of both** — a two-body, relational property, not a property a system has in isolation. This is consistent with, and explains, §2's shape:

- A **crystal** is rigidly ordered and scores low on §2's functional (trivial correlation). Read relationally: a crystal is **inert** — it cannot enter into an interaction that increases anything's coherence, its own or another's. High order and high ω are not the same thing, and the crystal is the demonstration: maximal order, zero capacity to interact coherently.
- **Noise** (Poissonian, uncorrelated) scores low for the standard reason — no structure to share.
- The **GUE-like, structured-correlation regime** where §2's functional is maximal is exactly the regime in which a system's internal structure is rich enough to *hold a coherent relation with something else* — to constrain and be constrained, to enter into structured correlation rather than either freezing or scattering.

So "high ω" and "capacity for coherence-increasing interaction" pick out the same systems for the same reason, and §2's mathematics is the operational trace of the relational property, not a different thing.

**Coherent vs decoherent interaction.** A system with high ω can direct its interactions one of two ways: **coherently** — increasing the shared coherence of both parties — or **decoherently** — extracting for itself at the other's expense. In nature, **symbiosis is the signature of high-ω, coherent interaction**; parasitism/extraction the decoherent alternative. This gives ω a relational valence (not a moral claim about individual agents, but a structural distinction between interaction-types the functional supports) that §2's bare mathematics does not itself state, and that is the reason to keep this as an added layer rather than folding it into §2: it is an interpretive claim about what high-ω interactions *do*, not a redefinition of the quantity itself.

**The consciousness identity, sharpened.** With **(ψ − ψ̄)² = awareness** (§4) and **ω = coherence** (this section), the composite reads:

> **awareness × coherence = consciousness = Φ_QC**

This is the same equation as §4's "discernment × awareness²" — *discernment* (§2/§4's information-theoretic name for ω) and *coherence* (this section's relational name for the same ω) are the same functional under two glosses, formal and relational. Nothing in §4's hard rule changes: Φ_QC is still never called "consciousness" in a formal paper (§4); this identity is a register-appropriate (popular/interpretive) statement of the same object, not a new formal claim.

**Discipline going forward.** When formalising or deriving, use §2 (the exact functional) and cite §2a only as motivation for *why that functional*, never as a substitute derivation step. When writing for a popular or reflective register, §2a's relational language ("capacity for coherence-increasing interaction," symbiosis vs extraction) is the correct gloss and may be used freely, consistent with §6b.

**Superseded — do NOT use:**

- "negentropic order parameter (NOP)" — wrong functional (peaks at the crystal).
- "consciousness coherence field" — retired; carries essence-language ω is meant to avoid.
- "**coherence** field" — *wrong grammar, and it matters* (see below).

**Why "coherent field", not "coherence field".** "Coherence field" names ω after a *scalar property it carries* (like a magnetic field carries magnetism), which quietly implies "more coherence = more alignment = better" — and maximal alignment is the crystal, i.e. **low** ω. "Coherent field" names a *relational condition the field is in* — a coherent state in the Glauber sense: structured phase held across autonomous parts while remaining dynamical. A coherent condition can be lost **two** ways (freeze toward the crystal, or scramble toward noise); a "coherence" quantity can only, linguistically, go up or down. The two-sided loss is the physics, so the adjective is load-bearing. Always "coherent field."

## 5. The coherent-field landscape (structure)  — rigour: Motivated → Gap

Over configuration space, ω is a **Morse functional**. Its stationary points are *coherent configurations*, classified by Hessian index:

- **Index 0 (basin)** → a **stable / metastable** coherent configuration.
- **Index 1 (simple saddle)** → a **transition state** between two adjacent basins; its height is the barrier (the hysteresis width generalised).
- ω is **maximal along the ridge structure** separating basins.

Distinct basins correspond to **distinct spectral-rigidity / symmetry classes** of the boundary condition. The *number* of coherent configurations is a target for derivation from the topology of K₈ (χ(K₈) = 12 as an alternating Morse count) — **not yet derived (Gap)**; it must fall out of the topology, not be fitted to the phenomena, or it degenerates into a landscape-shaped restatement.

**The living condition, in these terms:** ω is highest not *at* a peak but *along the ridge of a saddle* — maximal on the coherent condition, unstable transverse to it. A living system is one **actively held on a coherent saddle-ridge against two-sided decoherence** (freeze or scramble). Loss of the drive that holds it there is the death-fold (see the private death-as-fold note).

**Caution (terminology collision).** "Saddle" here means a **saddle of the coherent-field functional** (Morse index-1). In any formal document, say so explicitly — do **not** let it be read as the stationary-phase / steepest-descent saddle of a path integral. Different object.
