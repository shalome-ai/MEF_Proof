# Axiom audit

Every result listed below is machine-checked. This file records, for each
certified result, the complete set of foundational axioms its proof depends on —
traced transitively through every lemma it uses.

The output is generated by `#print axioms` during the continuous-integration
build, not asserted by the author. It can be reproduced by cloning the
repository and running `lake build`; the footprints appear in the build log.

## How to read this

Lean's kernel admits three axioms beyond its type theory:

- `propext` — propositional extensionality: propositions that imply each other
  are equal.
- `Classical.choice` — the axiom of choice.
- `Quot.sound` — soundness of quotient types.

These are the standard foundation of ordinary mathematics as formalised in Lean
and its library, and their appearance carries no adverse implication. A result
listed as depending on none of them has been proved constructively and without
choice.

What would be significant is the appearance of `sorryAx`, which marks an
incomplete proof, or of any axiom named outside the three above, which would
mark an assumption introduced by the author rather than by the foundations.

**Neither occurs anywhere in this file.**
